# Rail departures (release v0.1.0)

The default API base supplies STASY timetable departures at `/v1/rail/stasy/departures?stop=<name>&n=6`, the suburban station index at `/v1/rail/suburban/stations`, and boards at `/v1/rail/suburban/stations/{id}/departures?n=10`.

Station cards use the original GTFS station name. Explore → ISAP, metro, tram & suburban departures loads the rail explorer on demand. It offers M1 (ISAP), M2/M3 (metro), T6/T7 (tram), and suburban station selection. STASY selections filter the board to the chosen line and can show the route and stations on the map. Suburban boards include train number, origin, destination, scheduled time, status, nullable delay and platform. Boards refresh every 30 seconds for STASY and 60 seconds for suburban rail while visible, abort after 10 seconds and cancel on close or selection change. Requests share normal HTTP caches. Failures clear old boards and show an unavailable message. All departure times are displayed as supplied in Athens time.

STASY is scheduled data, with `indicative` warnings for expired timetables. Suburban boards preserve reported status, nullable delays and platforms; the backend may return a cached board during upstream failure. The transport explorer additionally uses /v1/rail/stasy/vehicles for explicitly labelled approximate timetable positions, and /v1/rail/suburban/vehicles for recent Attica suburban GPS positions. Feeds and GPS fixes older than two minutes are rejected; polls run every 15 seconds while visible. Selecting a train focuses and marks it on the map. The optional position adapter described in rail-realtime-feed.md remains separate.
